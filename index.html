What exactly do I pay?

£2,000 once, covering the strategy, build and implementation of your acquisition system. There is no Ophiro management fee for the first 60 days. Your advertising spend is paid directly to Google.

What happens after 60 days?

We review what was spent, how many enquiries came in, how many were qualified and what they turned into. If the economics work, we can move to an ongoing monthly partnership. If not, there is no lock-in and you keep the infrastructure we built.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. No long-term agency contract is needed to test the system.

How much ad budget do I need?

The Proof Offer is for established service businesses able to invest at least £2,000 per month in Google Ads. The right level depends on your market, competition and customer value, and we’ll advise on the call. The £2,000 implementation fee is separate from ad spend.

Do you guarantee results?

No. Search demand, competition, budget, offer strength and sales follow-up all influence outcomes, so we do not guarantee lead volumes, closed deals or revenue. That is exactly why there is a 60-day proof period before anything ongoing.
