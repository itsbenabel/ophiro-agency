What exactly do I pay?

£2,000 once, for the implementation of your complete acquisition system. There is no monthly management fee for the first 60 days. Your advertising spend is paid directly to Google.

What do I need to do?

Very little. One onboarding call, access to your accounts, and your sales criteria. We build the campaigns, landing page, qualification, response and tracking for you, with no checklist of tasks handed back to your team.

What happens after 60 days?

We review spend, qualified opportunities, cost per qualified opportunity, and the pipeline and revenue created. If the economics make sense, we discuss continuing the partnership. If not, there is no lock-in and you keep what we built.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. No long-term agency contract is needed to test the system.

How much ad budget do I need?

The build is designed for established service businesses with a £5,000+ monthly Google Ads budget and customers worth £3,000+ on average. We’ll confirm the right level for your market on the call. The £2,000 implementation fee is separate from ad spend.

Do you guarantee results?

We don’t guarantee sales volumes, because search demand, competition, budget, offer strength and your sales follow-up all influence outcomes. We do commit to our own behaviour: if, after 60 days, we haven’t demonstrated a viable path to qualified opportunities, we won’t ask you to continue onto a monthly retainer.
