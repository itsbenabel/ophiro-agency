Questions, answered

What growth leaders usually ask.

What counts as a high-ticket service business?

We mean businesses where one new customer is commercially valuable enough to support considered paid acquisition. Examples include professional services, specialist B2B providers, clinics, consultancies and high-value local services. The decisive factors are demand, margins, sales capacity and customer lifetime value.

Do you provide leads or manage the whole system?

We do not operate as a shared-lead seller. Ophiro builds and manages a direct system around your firm: campaigns, landing pages, qualification, response workflows, routing, pipeline visibility and attribution.

How much advertising budget do we need?

Most suitable businesses should be prepared to invest at least £5,000 per month in paid media. The appropriate level depends on your offer, search demand, competition, customer value and the data required for sound decisions. Strategy and infrastructure fees are separate.

Can you work with our existing CRM?

Often, yes. We first review whether it can support the required stages, source data, routing and outcome reporting. Where practical, we configure or integrate the existing system rather than forcing unnecessary replacement.

Can you track qualified calls and closed revenue?

Yes, where your CRM, call handling and team processes provide reliable data. We can connect source identifiers with agreed pipeline stages and report qualified opportunities, proposals and closed customers. Attribution quality depends on consistent data capture.

Do you guarantee leads or closed deals?

No. Search demand, competition, budget, offer strength, response speed and sales performance all influence outcomes. We optimise against the strongest reliable signals available, but do not guarantee lead volume, closed deals, revenue or return.

Can you work with our internal sales or marketing team?

Yes. Strong engagements usually involve close collaboration with leadership, marketing and sales. We define responsibilities, agree qualification standards and build a feedback loop so campaign decisions reflect what happens in real sales conversations.

Do you offer Meta advertising?

Google Search is the primary channel because it captures existing commercial intent. Meta may support retargeting or selected demand-generation activity where the audience, offer and economics make sense.
