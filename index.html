What if you’re ill or away?

My bots keep the routine work running every day: monitoring, alerts, reporting and lead tracking. Nothing changes in your account without me, so if I’m off sick for a day, your campaigns keep running as they are. They just don’t get new changes until I’m back. I give at least 2 weeks’ notice before any planned break, and I’m reachable on WhatsApp for real emergencies, like a broken campaign or a site that’s down. For any longer absence, which has never happened, I have a trusted Google Ads expert I’ve worked with personally who can step in.

Isn’t a bigger agency safer?

Bigger usually means your account gets run by a junior while the senior person sells to the next client. With me, you get the senior person every day. You own your ad account, campaigns and data from day one. I’m in Delhi, which means your account gets looked at before UK offices open.

What do the bots actually do?

The boring, repetitive work: daily spend and lead checks, search term reviews, flagging wasted spend, tracking where each enquiry came from, alerts when something breaks, reporting, and first drafts of ad copy and competitor research. I do the thinking: strategy, budgets, what to test and the final ad copy. A bot never changes your account without me approving it.
