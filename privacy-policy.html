Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Ophiro Agency Ltd ("Ophiro", "we", "us") is a UK-based marketing and AI agency. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and how we handle it, in line with UK GDPR.

Who we are

Ophiro Agency Ltd operates ophiro.agency and builds and manages digital marketing infrastructure - including landing pages, advertising campaigns, and CRM integrations - for our clients. Contact us at hello@ophiro.agency with any privacy questions.

Data we collect directly

If you book a call with us or email us directly, we collect the information you provide - typically your name, email address, and any details you share about your business. Call bookings are handled via Cal.com, a third-party scheduling provider; see Cal.com's privacy policy for how they process that data.

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. We do not run first-party tracking on ophiro.agency itself.

Data we process on behalf of clients

As part of delivering marketing services, Ophiro builds and operates technical integrations for clients - for example, connecting a client's website to their CRM, or reporting lead-conversion events back to an advertising platform (such as Google Ads) so campaign performance can be measured accurately. In this capacity, Ophiro acts as a data processor on behalf of our clients, who remain the data controller for their own customers' and leads' personal data. We only process that data as instructed by the relevant client, and only for the purpose of delivering the agreed service (e.g. attributing a qualified lead back to the advertising click that generated it).

We do not sell, rent, or use client end-customer data for any purpose beyond delivering the contracted service.

Third-party services we use

  • Cal.com - call scheduling
  • Vercel - website hosting
  • Google Ads / Google Data Manager API - advertising campaign management and conversion reporting, on behalf of clients
  • GoHighLevel - client relationship management, on behalf of clients

Data retention

We retain contact details you provide directly to us for as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry or maintain our business relationship, and delete them on request. Client end-customer data is retained according to the relevant client's own policies and instructions.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact hello@ophiro.agency. If your enquiry relates to a specific client's customer data (for example, a lead submitted through a landing page we built), please contact that business directly, as they are the data controller.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
